The invitation

What happens after you apply?

Joining a collective is a considered, mutual journey. We take the time to understand the fit—on both sides.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Tell us what draws you here and the life you hope to build.

  2. 02

    We review

    We consider your responses, readiness and alignment with the collective.

  3. 03

    We meet

    If there is mutual alignment, we arrange a 1-on-1 in-person conversation.

  4. 04

    Next steps

    We close the loop with a clear outcome and the way forward.

Your application begins the conversation. It does not confirm membership.
