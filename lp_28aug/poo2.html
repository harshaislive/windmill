The path to membership

A considered path to joining Poomaale 2.0

Becoming part of a Beforest collective is a considered, mutual decision. Here is what the journey looks like before you begin.

  1. 01

    Application

    Tell us what brings you here, what you hope to build, and how you see yourself participating.

  2. 02

    Review

    Our team considers your responses, readiness, and alignment with the collective's values and current offering.

  3. 03

    1-on-1 conversation

    If there is mutual alignment, we contact you to agree on a convenient time and place to meet in person.

  4. 04

    Closure & next steps

    After the conversation, we clearly confirm the outcome and what happens next.

What happens next?

Once you submit, our team will carefully review your responses. Please answer thoughtfully and as specifically as you can—your responses help us understand the fit. Submitting an application begins the review; it does not confirm membership yet.
