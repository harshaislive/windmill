What changed after the Typeform and brochure flow update

Leads now move from Typeform to WhatsApp more directly. Windmill still logs everything in Pipedrive, sends brochure links through Interakt when needed, and creates owner tasks when a lead completes the post-brochure form.

Known Caveats

Useful when someone asks "why did this not send?"

Interakt templates are pending approval

The new brochure and post-brochure acknowledgement templates were created, but were pending Meta approval at creation/read-back. Brochure send has a legacy template fallback.

WhatsApp redirect cannot confirm send

Typeform can redirect to WhatsApp, but cannot force the lead to press send. That is why the 5-minute fallback exists.

Duplicate protection is activity-based

Workers check Pipedrive activities before sending brochure or acknowledgement again. Do not manually delete audit activities unless you want to remove dedupe evidence.
