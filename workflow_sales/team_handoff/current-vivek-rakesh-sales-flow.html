What remains common

Pipedrive is the source of truth

Existing Hammiyala deals are never rebalanced. Every later automation follows the deal’s current owner.

WhatsApp failure has a fallback

Eligible failed nurture messages are picked up by the daily Mailchimp rescue flow.

Every write is deduplicated

Stable activity subjects and booking references prevent repeat updates and duplicate activities.

Legacy prebook is not the default

calendar_prebook_1on1 remains internal/manual and is not called for fit intake leads.
