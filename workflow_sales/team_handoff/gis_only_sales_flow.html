GIS-Only Sales Funnel

The webinar path is replaced by a Collective Journey-first path. Leads either qualify for team-managed conversation follow-up, or they are sent the relevant Collective Journey and asked to request a conversation from the form at the end of the brochure.

Typeform Windmill Pipedrive WhatsApp / Interakt Collective Journey Disabled legacy

1. Intake

Primary collective Typeforms

Bhopal, Mumbai, Poomaale 2.0, and Hammiyala intake forms send submissions to Windmill.

↓
f/sales/typeform_to_pipedrive_intake

Parses answers, normalizes contact fields, resolves the collective, and checks the why-join answer.

↓
Same-deal update priority

Hidden pipedrive_deal_id wins. Then response-token guard, then same person/contact in same collective. New deal only if no safe match exists.

2. Routing Decision

Meaningful why-join + fit

Deal gets existing 1on1 TBC label and receives a team scheduling notice. No calendar slot is prebooked automatically.

↓
Blank, missing, or filler why-join

Deal is labelled GIS Awaiting. No Calendly or Zoom link is sent.

↓
Unfit or manual fallback

Deal is labelled GIS Awaiting. Azure uncertainty now falls back to GIS rather than webinar.

3. Pipedrive Guard

Deal label webhook

Pipedrive sends deal changes to the legacy route pipedrive/1on1/tbc/prebook. The route name is old; the live action is the team scheduling notice.

↓
f/sales/pipedrive_1on1_tbc_to_calendar_prebook

If 1on1 TBC, it queues the 1on1 team scheduling notice. If GIS Awaiting, it queues the GIS CTA sender.

↓
f/sales/send_1on1_team_scheduling_notice

Sends a concise WhatsApp note saying one of the team members will message directly to schedule the 1on1.

↓
f/sales/send_gis_story_cta

Builds a signed tracking token, sends the WhatsApp template, logs GIS story link sent, and applies GIS Sent.

4. WhatsApp CTA

Template

collective_story_initial_cta_v3

↓
Message intent

Go through the Collective Journey in full first. Request a conversation from the form at the end of the brochure.

↓
Button URL

https://windmill.devsharsha.live/api/r/sales/gis/open?t={{token}}

5. GIS Open Tracking And Post-GIS Request

Open tracker

f/sales/track_gis_story_open verifies the signed token, redirects to ArcGIS, and best-effort logs GIS story opened plus GIS Opened. Deleted Pipedrive deals must not block the lead from opening the Journey.

Collective Journey

The lead goes through the Collective Journey. The bottom CTA sends them to the matching post-GIS Typeform.

Post-GIS Typeform

Journey bottom CTAs use fixed Windmill redirect URLs under sales/gis/request-1on1/{collective}, then forward collective, source=collective_story, and story_cta=bottom_1on1_request into Typeform.

6. Collective Journey Second-Form Nurture

Audience

Deals with GIS Sent or GIS Opened that have not submitted the post-Journey Typeform. Opening the journey does not stop the sequence.

Cadence

Seven alternate-day WhatsApp follow-ups on Day 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, and 14 after the first Collective Journey send.

f/sales/send_collective_story_unopened_nurture

Uses tracked buttons, collective-specific context lines, and Pipedrive activities to dedupe every follow-up step. The sequence stops after Post-GIS Typeform submitted.

Operational Notes

Windmill/Pipedrive click tracking is the operational source of truth for whether a lead opened the Collective Journey. ArcGIS or GA4 analytics can still be used for aggregate reading behavior, but the CRM state comes from the signed Windmill redirect.
