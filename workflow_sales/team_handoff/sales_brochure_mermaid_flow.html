Sales Summary

1

Lead fills Typeform

Typeform sends the lead to WhatsApp and sends the submission to Windmill.

2

Windmill creates Pipedrive trail

Person and deal are created or updated. Sales sees Typeform received.

3

Brochure is sent

If the lead sends the WhatsApp message, brochure is sent immediately. If not, Windmill waits 5 minutes and sends it.

4

Conversation happens after brochure

The lead requests a conversation from the form at the end of the brochure. Owner gets a Pipedrive task.

What Sales Sees In Pipedrive

Activity Meaning Sales action
Typeform received The first Typeform landed and the deal/person was created or updated. Use this as the start of the automation trail.
Brochure sent after WhatsApp redirect The lead sent the prefilled WhatsApp message, Interakt inbound was received, and the brochure was sent. No manual brochure send needed.
Brochure fallback sent after Typeform The lead did not send the WhatsApp message within the fallback window, so Windmill sent the brochure anyway. No manual brochure send needed.
GIS story link sent Compatibility label activity for the brochure CTA path. It now sends Canva brochure links using collective_brochure_link_v2. Treat this as brochure sent.
Brochure opened The lead clicked the tracked brochure button and reached the Canva brochure. Good signal, but wait for the post-brochure Typeform unless the owner wants to intervene.
Post-brochure Typeform submitted The lead requested a conversation after going through the brochure. Owner should act on the follow-up task.
Review post-brochure conversation request Owner task created from the second form. Message or call the lead. This is the main sales handoff point.
Post-brochure acknowledgement sent The lead was told that the owner will reach out soon. Owner should not delay; the customer has already been promised follow-up.
Send brochure manually Automatic WhatsApp send failed or phone/template data was missing. Use the copy-paste message in the activity note.

What To Remember

Do not send old GIS links

Customer-facing links are Canva brochure links. Old GIS/StoryMap labels remain only for internal compatibility.

Do not ask for a reply as the next step

The current next step is the conversation request form at the end of the brochure.

Do not duplicate the brochure

Windmill checks Pipedrive activities before sending. If a brochure was already sent, it skips duplicate sends.

Manual tasks mean automation needs help

If Pipedrive shows a manual send task, use the exact copy-paste text inside the note.

The second form is the clean handoff

Once the lead submits it, sales should treat the lead as ready for a conversation.
