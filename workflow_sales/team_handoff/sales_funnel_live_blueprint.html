Beforest sales funnel: brochure, WhatsApp, nurture, and fallback flow

This is the operating map for what happens after a collective Typeform is submitted. The core rule is simple: every eligible lead should get the right collective brochure/Journey, the owner should see exactly what happened in Pipedrive, and failed WhatsApp delivery should have a backup path.

At A Glance

Use this section when explaining the flow quickly before going into the detailed chart.

Primary intake
Typeform to Pipedrive
Immediate user action
Redirect to WhatsApp
Main asset
Canva brochure links
Fallback
Interakt + Mailchimp

1. User submits primary Typeform

Windmill creates or updates the Pipedrive person/deal, maps the collective, logs the Typeform details, and applies the correct route: 1on1-fit or brochure-first.

2. Typeform ending opens WhatsApp

The Typeform ending redirects to wa.me/918977945351 with a prefilled message. Fit and brochure-first endings can use different text, but both point to WhatsApp.

3. Brochure delivery is protected

If the user sends the WhatsApp message, Interakt inbound triggers brochure delivery. If they do not send it, Windmill sends the brochure after the fallback window.

Timing And Automation Schedule

These are the live timing rules that matter for sales operations.

Immediately
Typeform webhook creates/updates Pipedrive, logs intake, and queues the brochure fallback. Typeform itself redirects the user to WhatsApp.
After inbound WhatsApp
Interakt webhook resolves the deal by phone and sends the correct brochure if not already sent.
After 5 minutes
If no WhatsApp inbound was received after Typeform submission, Windmill sends the brochure fallback through Interakt and logs the result.
8 AM, 12 PM, 7 PM, 10 PM
Mailchimp fallback for primary Typeform leads whose WhatsApp brochure delivery failed and still has no inbound WhatsApp/manual resolution.
10 AM daily
Mailchimp fallback for failed day-wise Journey nurture WhatsApps, grouped by collective and nurture day.
10:30 AM daily
Collective Journey nurture sends day 2/4/6/8/10/12/14 follow-ups for active deals until post-brochure form is submitted.

Edge Cases Covered

These guards are what keep the system from double-sending or hiding failures.

No phone number

Windmill does not attempt a WhatsApp send. It creates a Pipedrive manual-review activity so the owner knows the lead needs manual handling.

Multiple deals with same phone

The resolver prefers the latest recent open deal in the same collective. If matching is ambiguous, the safe outcome is manual review rather than a blind send.

User replies after fallback

Inbound is logged but brochure send is deduped by Pipedrive activity, so the lead should not get duplicate brochure messages.

Post-brochure form submitted early

The post-brochure path stops Journey nurture and sends the owner acknowledgement instead of continuing fallback nudges.

Closed or deleted deal

Workers stop or skip sends after checking the deal state, reducing waste and avoiding messages for stale deals.

Template, email, or API failure

Failures are logged back to Pipedrive as manual-send or email-fallback-failed activities, so the owner can still act.
