TYPEFORM

Lead submits collective intake form

Bhopal, Mumbai, Poomaale 2.0, or Hammiyala form lands in Windmill and updates or creates the Pipedrive deal.

|
CRM

Windmill matches the right deal

Hidden deal ID wins. Otherwise it checks duplicate response token, then same person/contact in the same collective. New deal only if no safe match exists.

|
ROUTER

Why-join answer decides the path

Meaningful answer moves toward conversation qualification. Missing, blank, filler, unfit, or fallback moves to the Collective Journey path.

PATH A: qualified 1on1 lead

WhatsApp sent: one of our team members will message directly to schedule the 1on1.
Sales rep action: follow up manually and schedule the 1on1. This is not auto-booked.

PATH B: Collective Journey first

WhatsApp sent with tracked button: Open Collective Journey.
Lead reads story and requests a 1on1 from the bottom CTA if interested.

Manual checkpoint: qualified lead follow-up

When a lead receives the 1on1 team scheduling notice, the sales team must message them personally and schedule the conversation. The automation intentionally does not prebook a calendar slot.

|
STORY

Collective Journey path

Lead is sent a Collective Journey. Internally this may still be tracked as GIS, but external copy says Collective Journey.

|
TRACK

Open is tracked through Windmill

The WhatsApp button goes through a signed Windmill URL first. Pipedrive moves from GIS Sent to GIS Opened, then redirects to the Collective Journey.

|
IF SECOND FORM NOT SUBMITTED

14-day second-form nurture

For leads with GIS Sent or GIS Opened who have not submitted the post-Journey Typeform, WhatsApp follow-ups run on Day 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, and 14. The sequence stops after the second form is submitted.

|
BOTTOM CTA

Lead requests 1on1 from the story

Each Collective Journey bottom CTA uses a fixed Windmill redirect and forwards hidden Typeform fields such as collective, source, and story_cta.

|
POST-STORY FORM

Post-story Typeform updates the CRM

Windmill updates the same Pipedrive deal when it can safely match it. If not, it uses contact and collective fallback matching before creating anything new.

|

Manual checkpoint: post-Journey conversation request

When someone requests a conversation after going through the Collective Journey, sales should review the deal context, message the lead personally, and move the conversation forward. The automation prepares the CRM context; the rep owns the conversation.

|
DISABLED

Old webinar path is off

No webinar CTA, no Calendly webinar registration, no Zoom registration, no webinar reminders, and no webinar attendance sync for this funnel.

Sales Must Do

  • Follow up with qualified leads after the team scheduling notice.
  • Follow up when a post-Journey conversation request comes in.
  • Use Pipedrive context before messaging the lead.

Automation Does

  • Routes intake to direct conversation or Collective Journey.
  • Sends approved WhatsApp templates.
  • Tracks opens and updates Pipedrive state labels.
  • Runs second-form nurture for Journey leads until the post-Journey Typeform is submitted.

Do Not Use

  • Do not send webinar links for this path.
  • Do not rely on automatic calendar prebooking.
  • Do not create duplicate deals if a safe CRM match exists.
Team rule: Automation creates context and timing. Sales reps create the actual relationship. When the system says a team member will reach out, the team must reach out.
